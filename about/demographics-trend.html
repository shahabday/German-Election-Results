The same demographic and economic indicators as the map tool, but as click-to-compare trend lines.

Demographics — Time Series

Compare states' or counties' economic and demographic indicators over time — the same click-map, hover-preview, or search-by-name comparison tool used by the election time series, applied to the 14 county-level metrics from the demographics map (income, GDP, unemployment, and more). Useful paired with the election trends map for "why" questions — e.g. checking whether a swing toward a party in a region lines up with a real wage or unemployment trend there.

State-level lines are a simple, unweighted average of that state's counties — a small rural county counts exactly as much as a big city within its state, since there's no consistent multi-year population-weighting dataset available across this whole period.
