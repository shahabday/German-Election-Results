Bundestag Election

Germany's parliament, chosen nationwide

Every four years, the whole country votes on the same day for the Bundestag. This is the most recent result, municipality by municipality.

Open the Bundestag map →
Landtag Election

Sixteen states, sixteen calendars

Each state elects its own parliament on its own schedule, not all at once. Our tools always show every state at its own most recent vote, which is exactly what this map does.

Open the Landtag map →
Kommunalwahl

Local councils, closest to home

Municipal councils are also elected state by state, on their own staggered schedules. Again, every area here is shown at its own most recent vote.

Open the Kommunalwahl map →
Europawahl

One ballot, shared across Europe

Every five years, Germany votes alongside the rest of the EU for the European Parliament, all on the same day, nationwide.

Germany holds some election almost every year, nationwide or local, as you just saw. This tool combines all of them into one always-current picture, so you can watch a party rise and fall over time, not just at four snapshots a decade apart.
