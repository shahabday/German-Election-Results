Correlation Explorer

Pick any two of the ~29 county-level metrics from across this project — economy, health, family, migration, demographics — and see how they relate across all 402 counties. Each point is one county; the dashed line is the least-squares fit, and r is the Pearson correlation coefficient. Correlation is not causation - many of these pairs move together because both track something else (income, urban density, East/West history), not because one causes the other.

Each variable uses its own most recent available year (shown in the axis title) — since coverage ranges from a single 2019 survey to 2025, the two axes of a given chart are not always the same reference year. Points are colored by state; several variables (church membership, sports club membership, life satisfaction, volunteering, life expectancy, tax capacity's underlying Gini/S80-S20, health personnel density, renewable electricity share) are only published at STATE level and broadcast to every county in that state, so they form tight horizontal or vertical bands of identical values rather than county-by-county variation — that's expected, not a bug. Data combines every metric already built for the Society & Culture map plus two age-group shares (under 15, 65+) computed from the population-by-age-group tool.
