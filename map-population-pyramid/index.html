Population Pyramid

How old is Germany, and how has that changed? Each bar is a 5-year age band; blue (left) is men, orange (right) is women. Pick a state or all of Germany, then scrub the year slider or hit play to watch the population age.

Source: regionalstatistik.de, table 12411-04-02-4-B ("Bevölkerung nach Geschlecht und Altersjahren", Stichtag 31.12.). Ages 0-74 are counted year by year and rolled up here into standard 5-year bands (0-4, 5-9, ... 85-89), with 90+ as an open top band. Covers 2011, 2014, 2017, 2020, 2023 and 2025 — a representative subset of the full annual series rather than every single year, chosen to show the shape of the demographic shift (population growth, the aging baby-boom cohort, below-replacement birth rates) without an exhaustive per-year pull. "Deutschland" is the sum of all 16 states from the same source, not a separately published national figure.
